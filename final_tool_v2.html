Liquid stocks (n=20): GARCH dominates, not EGARCH-X

Theory recommended EGARCH-X for liquid stocks, but GARCH(1,1) wins 19 out of 20. Only stock 119 went to EGARCH-X. Intraday 10-minute sessions are too short to stably estimate the BAS spread coefficient — EGARCH-X needs longer windows. Median QLIKE: GARCH −7.05 vs EGARCH-X −7.02 vs LightGBM −6.83.

◆

Illiquid stocks (n=20): LightGBM wins all 20 — HAR-RV never wins

Theory recommended HAR-RV for illiquid stocks, but LightGBM wins every single one (0% theory agreement). HAR-RV assumes linear persistence in RV lags, but sparse order books generate non-linear volatility dynamics that gradient boosting captures. Median QLIKE: LightGBM −5.94 vs GARCH −5.54 vs HAR-RV −5.47.

▸

Mixed stocks (n=20): LightGBM wins 16/20 — theory mostly correct here

Theory recommended LightGBM for mixed stocks, and it wins 16 out of 20 (80% agreement). GARCH wins the remaining 4 stocks (IDs: 56, 76, 113, 122). Mixed regime is where the pipeline recommendation is most reliable. Median QLIKE: LightGBM −6.44 vs GARCH −6.38.
